Privacy

The portfolio pages do not provide forms, account creation, or file uploads. The site source contains no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request logs.

Separate demo applications

Links to Pulseboard and Knowledge Assistant open separate applications hosted on their own services. Their data processing is separate from these static portfolio pages.

  • Pulseboard issues visitors a temporary session and stores board data through its API. Demo data may reset when the service restarts.
  • Knowledge Assistant lets visitors ask about a built-in sample; an account is required to upload documents. The demo does not provide email verification or password recovery, and data may reset. Do not upload confidential documents or personal data.

GitHub, Telegram, and other linked platforms process data under their own policies. Share data only if you agree to the relevant service's terms.
