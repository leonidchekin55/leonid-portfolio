HOW IT WORKS

Free-tier data recovery

Database and media updates are encrypted and saved to a separate private GitHub repository. Uploaded files are not public. Snapshot history is compacted after ten saves to limit its size.

This is a small demo: the encrypted media archive is capped at 20 MiB and expanded data at 64 MiB. Render Free may sleep. Real payments, fulfillment, and email campaigns are not configured.
